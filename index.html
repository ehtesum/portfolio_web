About Me

EUI

I'm a Software Engineer & AI Systems Researcher based in Finland 🇫🇮, holding a Master of Science in Software Engineering & Digital Transformation from LUT University (completed December 2025) and a B.Sc. in Computer Science & Engineering from North South University (2021).

With 2+ years of professional software engineering and cloud experience, I bridge the gap between robust production systems and experimental AI. My core technical expertise spans Python (Django, FastAPI, Flask), PostgreSQL, Microsoft Azure, Docker, and automated CI/CD pipelines.

My current research focuses on Reliable & Adaptive AI Systems — specifically multi-agent and multi-model multimodal inference, runtime model orchestration, verification, knowledge graphs, and selective abstention to eliminate hallucinations.
